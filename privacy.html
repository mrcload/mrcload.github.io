PRIVACY POLICY

version 3 - applies to the Google Play build (com.drew.crystalball)

This game does not require an account and does not run a server.

WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Progress, energy, rating, crystals, cosmetics you own and your

settings are saved in a local file. They never leave the device.

LOCAL TWO-PHONE PLAY

Linked matches connect two devices directly over your own network.

Only gameplay state is sent - timing, scores, spells cast. Nothing is

relayed through us, and nothing is stored after the match.

ADVERTISING

When ads are enabled in this build they are served by Google

AdMob, which may collect device identifiers. That is the only

data that leaves your device, and Google's policy governs it.

Where ad consent was asked, change it in SETTINGS > PRIVACY.

CHILDREN

This game is not directed at children.
